02 / THE OTHER SIDE

Not every love story stays.

Some endings hurt because what existed mattered. Heartbreak is not the whole story. It is one chapter in a life that keeps moving.

AFTER THE END

There are things
we only understand

Missing someone can make a room feel different. A song can become a place. A familiar street can suddenly contain an entire history.

02.01

Missing someone

Sometimes you miss the person. Sometimes you miss the version of yourself that existed around them. Both can be true.

02.02

When things change

People grow. Relationships do too. Not every change is a betrayal, and not every ending erases what came before it.

02.03

Letting go

Letting go can be an act of care for yourself and for what once was. It can make space without pretending the space was never occupied.

FIELD NOTE

Grief and gratitude can live in the same room.

We sometimes imagine healing as forgetting. But memory does not always need to disappear for life to become lighter.

You can be grateful for what happened and still know that it needed to end. You can miss someone and still choose forward motion.

The heart is capable of holding contradictions. That may be one of its strangest strengths.
