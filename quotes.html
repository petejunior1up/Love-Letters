WHY WORDS STAY

A line can become a tiny place you return to.

A good sentence does not have to explain everything. Sometimes it simply names one part of an experience with enough accuracy that you recognize yourself inside it.

That is why people save lines in notes, margins, screenshots and memory. The words become a shorthand for something larger.

THE LOVE ARCHIVE / MARGIN NOTE 05.09
READ SLOWLY

Words change depending on what you have lived.

FIRST READ

You hear the sentence.

A phrase may simply sound beautiful, sharp or true.

LATER

You recognize the experience.

Time can give old words a new meaning because you are no longer the same reader.

KEEP

You make it yours.

The line becomes connected to your own memory, not because the writer knew your life, but because language gave your life somewhere to meet it.
