LETTER 004

UNSENT / SOMEWHERE BETWEEN THEN AND NOW

To the person who taught me that being known can feel like home.

I do not know whether you ever realized how many ordinary moments became important because you were there.

That is the strange thing about memory. It does not always preserve the grandest day. Sometimes it keeps a doorway, a joke, the sound of someone saying your name, the exact feeling of being understood for a moment.

I hope you know that what mattered was never perfection. It was presence.

And if the story changed, that does not make the earlier chapters false.

— An unfinished thought

LETTER 005

To whoever I love next

I hope we learn each other slowly. I hope we never confuse certainty with knowing everything.

Let us keep asking questions even after we think we know the answers. Let us be kind when one of us is still learning how to be understood.

LETTER 006

To the version of me who stayed soft

Thank you for believing that tenderness was not weakness.

You did not have to become colder to become wiser. Keep the softness. Just learn where it belongs.
