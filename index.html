never said.

Short letters for the words that get stuck somewhere between the heart and the mouth.

LETTER 001

To the person I never told

There are people who become important before we know what to call the feeling. Maybe that is why the memory lasts.

I wonder whether you ever noticed the little ways I tried to say it without saying it. Maybe the unfinished sentence was the most honest thing I could give you.

LETTER 002

To whoever I love next

I hope we learn each other slowly. I hope we never confuse certainty with knowing everything.

Let us keep asking questions even after we think we know the answers. Let us be kind when one of us is still learning how to be understood.

LETTER 003

To the version of me who stayed

Thank you for believing that tenderness was not weakness, and that being honest was worth the risk.

You did not have to become colder to become wiser. Keep the softness. Just learn where it belongs.

The letters are complete. The stories are not.

THOUGHTS

Small truths
about love.

Some ideas are too simple to be clichés and too complicated to be rules.

01

Love notices.

It remembers the small things because the small things are usually where a person lives.

02

Love needs room.

Closeness is not the same as ownership. Healthy connection leaves space for two whole people.

03

Love changes.

A feeling can become trust, a habit, a memory, a friendship, or something entirely new.

04

Love asks.

Good intentions are not mind-reading. Sometimes the most loving question is simply: “What do you need?”
